## Selected Research & Computational Projects

### Anubis — Ancient Egyptian Collagen Peptide Database
An interactive scientific platform for **in-silico digestion, peptide-to-species mapping, ZooMS marker exploration, and paleoproteomics analysis**.

**Tools:** Python · R · Shiny · Bioinformatics

### Automated Breast Ultrasound Analysis
An end-to-end deep-learning workflow for **lesion segmentation, localization, and benign–malignant classification** from breast ultrasound images.

**Methods:** CNNs · ResNet · EfficientNet · DenseNet · Vision Transformers

### Single-Cell Transcriptomics
Reproducible workflows for analyzing single-cell RNA-seq datasets, including **quality control, normalization, dimensionality reduction, clustering, integration, and biological interpretation**.

**Tools:** R · Seurat v5 · SCTransform · RPCA

### FOXA1 in Breast Cancer
Bioinformatics investigation of genetic and post-transcriptional regulation of **FOXA1 in breast cancer**, including SNP and miRNA analyses.
